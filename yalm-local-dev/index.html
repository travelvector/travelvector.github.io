Yalm Local Dev

A personal development and Google Workspace integration operated by Yalm (GitHub: travelvector).

Local tools and user-directed AI assistants use this integration to work with the operator's Google Workspace account. Google Drive access supports finding, reading, creating and updating files. Gmail, Calendar, Docs and Sheets permissions support reading requested messages, calendars, documents and spreadsheets. Account profile and email identify the authorized account.

The integration also supports personal development with Google Cloud services. Google Workspace access is granted through OAuth; each account owner controls whether to grant or revoke access. This is a personal integration, not a public sign-up service or a Google product.
